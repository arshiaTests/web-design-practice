Chocolate Cake Recipe

This delicious chocolate cake is rich, moist, and perfect for birthdays, celebrations, or any time you want a sweet treat.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Mix the flour, sugar, and cocoa powder in a bowl.
  3. Add the eggs and milk, then stir until smooth.
  4. Pour the batter into a baking pan.
  5. Bake for 30-35 minutes.
  6. Let the cake cool before serving.
